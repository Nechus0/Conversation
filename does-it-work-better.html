Does It Work Better?

Find out how many people you need to fairly compare a group that gets something new with a group that doesn't.

Example: Does a text-message reminder help more parents bring their child back for the second vaccine dose? Without a reminder, 4 in 10 parents come back. With a reminder, we hope for 6 in 10.

A learning aid, not a substitute for a statistician. Two-sided test, 5% significance level.
